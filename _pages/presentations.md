Talks and recorded presentations.

* [Safety for the First Billion Agents (AI Safety Forum 2026)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fs53HjWsYaY): Current AI safety is not enough to prevent extreme power concentration, gradual disempowerment, and resource contention with social-scale agentic systems. Safety needs a new approach which acknowledges the unique challenges and tooling of large agent systems, from efficiently scaling existing techniques to developing infrastructure which adapts to its participants. I will discuss new demands of large agent systems, compare nascent approaches, and note barriers to progress. I will conclude by sharing a community and centralised resources for interested researchers.
