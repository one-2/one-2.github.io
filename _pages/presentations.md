Talks and recorded presentations.

* 8 September 2026 - [Large Agent Systems Safety (Bondi Innovation)](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1e5FziGx69zDMMkC0mHlIqFBPcvXWVom8FJ115eT43IM/edit?usp=sharing): I introduce topics in large agent systems safety to an audience of applied AI engineers and entrepreneurs.
* 8 July 2026 - [Safety for the First Billion Agents (AI Safety Forum 2026)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fs53HjWsYaY): Current AI safety is not enough to prevent extreme power concentration, gradual disempowerment, and resource contention with social-scale agentic systems. Safety needs a new approach which acknowledges the unique challenges and tooling of large agent systems, from efficiently scaling existing techniques to developing infrastructure which adapts to its participants. I discuss new demands of large agent systems, compare nascent approaches, and note barriers to progress. I conclude by sharing a community and centralised resources for interested researchers.
