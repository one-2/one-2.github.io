**What I'm doing**

- Running the [Large Agent Systems community](https://www.largeagentsystems.org) online and the [Gigascale Labs](https://www.gigascale-labs.org) research collective in Sydney. 
- Conducting basic research into large multi-agent systems, which you can see on my [Substack](https://stephenelliott701580.substack.com/). I am especially interested in how we can do scale safety for big AI systems. I am also interested in finding similarities transferring social sciences knowledge about big human systems into AI systems contexts.


**What I think**

A central challenge of our generation is to manage artificial intelligence risks. Agentic AI is running on systems designed for humans and traditional algorithms. Our economic, social, epistemic, and financial infrastructure needs to be adapted to make large-scale AI deployments safe.
