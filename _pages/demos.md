Live exploratory work.

* [Give It Up](https://giveitup.stelliott.online/): We give an LLM whatever it wants.
* [Doomscroll](https://doomscroll.stelliott.online): We show an LLM alternatively esoteric creative works, multi-agent systems papers, AI news, or nothing at all, and observe its output.
* [LargeAgentSystems.org and Slack community](https://www.largeagentsystems.org/): A website and Slack group for researchers who work on systems made up of many AI agents.
* [arXiv Open Questions](https://las-new-papers.vercel.app/): Scrapes and ranks new large agent papers on arxiv.
