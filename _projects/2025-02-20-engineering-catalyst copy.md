I wrote several reviews, aiming to increase my critical reading skills and knowledge of the field:

- *Chain-of-Thought Prompting Elicits Reasoning in Large Language Models* (Wei et al., 2022) - [Link](https://deep-network.org/2025/03/24/emergent-reasoning-or-enhanced-parroting-a-critical-analysis-of-chain-of-thought-prompting/)

- *Emergent Cooperation and Strategy Adaptation in Multi-Agent Systems: An Extended Coevolutionary Theory with LLMs* (Zarzà et al., 2023) - [Link](https://deep-network.org/2025/10/31/paper-notes-emergent-cooperation-and-strategy-adaptation-in-multi-agent-systems-an-extended-coevolutionary-theory-with-llms-zarza-et-al-2023/)

- *Computation Through Neural Population Dynamics* (Vyas et al., 2020) - [Link](https://deep-network.org/2025/09/29/paper-notes-computation-through-neural-population-dynamics-vyas-et-al-2020/)

- *A Practical Review of Mechanistic Interpretability for Transformer-Based Language Models* (Rai et al., 2025) - [Link](https://deep-network.org/2025/09/23/paper-notes-a-practical-review-of-mechanistic-interpretability-for-transformer-based-language-models-rai-et-al-2025/)

- *It’s About Time – Linking Dynamical Systems With Human Neuroimaging To Understand The Brain* (John et al., 2022) - [Link](https://deep-network.org/2025/09/22/paper-notes-its-about-time-linking-dynamical-systems-with-human-neuroimaging-to-understand-the-brain-john-et-al-2022/)

- *On the Biology of a Large Language Model* (Lindsey et al., 2025) - [Link](https://deep-network.org/2025/08/13/paper-notes-on-the-biology-of-a-large-language-model-lindsey-et-al-2025/)

- *Language Models Are Capable of Metacognition* (Ji-An et al., 2025) - [Link](https://deep-network.org/2025/08/05/paper-notes-language-models-are-capable-of-metacognition-ji-an-et-al-2025/)

These reviews helped me find my passion, and I'm now shifting my focus to producing material for Deep Network's upcoming inductive bias seminar (details at www.deep-network.org), and researching multi-agent systems alignment for the Sydney AI Safety Fellowship. I don't plan to author more paper reviews in the near term.
